About Our Age Calculator

Creator Toolkit's free age calculator helps you calculate your age using your date of birth.

The calculator displays your age in years, months and days, making it easy to understand your exact current age.

The tool works directly in your web browser and does not require any software installation.

How to Use the Age Calculator

Step 1: Select Your Date of Birth

Select your date of birth using the date picker.

Step 2: Calculate Your Age

Click the Calculate Age button.

Step 3: View Your Age

Your age will be displayed in years, months and days.
