About Our Calculator

Creator Toolkit's free online calculator helps you perform everyday mathematical calculations.

You can perform addition, subtraction, multiplication, division and percentage calculations.

How to Use the Calculator

Step 1

Click the numbers you want to calculate.

Step 2

Select an operator such as +, −, × or ÷.

Step 3

Click = to see the result.

Step 4

Use C to clear the calculation or ⌫ to delete the last character.
