About Our Word Counter

Our free online word counter helps you quickly count the number of words and characters in your text.

It can also count sentences and paragraphs, making it useful for students, writers, bloggers, creators and anyone who works with text.

How to Use the Word Counter

Step 1

Type or paste your text into the text box.

Step 2

The tool will automatically calculate your word, character, sentence and paragraph counts.

Step 3

Use the Clear Text button to remove the text and start again.
