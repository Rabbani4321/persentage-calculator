How to Calculate Percentage

To calculate a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100.

For example, 20% of 500 is 100.

Percentage Formula

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate a percentage?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply the result by 100.

What is 20% of 500?

How do you calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide the result by the old value, and multiply by 100.
