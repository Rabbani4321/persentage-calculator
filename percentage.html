Percentage Formula

The basic percentage formula is:

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

For example, if you scored 80 marks out of 100 marks:

(80 ÷ 100) × 100 = 80%

Percentage Calculation Examples

Example 1: Find a percentage of a number

20% of 500 = (20 ÷ 100) × 500 = 100

Example 2: Find what percentage one number is of another

25 out of 100 = (25 ÷ 100) × 100 = 25%

Example 3: Calculate percentage change

If a value changes from 100 to 120, the percentage change is 20%.
